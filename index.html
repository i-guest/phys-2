Chapter 1: Electric forces (Coulomb's law)

Force between two point charges: F = k q1q2 / r². Like charges repel, unlike charges attract, and doubling the distance cuts the force to ¼. Drag the blue buddy along the ruler.

E = F/q0 = kq/r², and fields add as vectors (superposition). Tap the paper to add charges, drag them around, and drag the yellow pin to read E and V anywhere. Field lines leave + charges and end on − charges.

Chapter 3: Gauss's law

Φ = ∮ E·dA = Qin / ε0. Only the charge inside the Gaussian surface counts, wherever it sits. Drag the buddies across the dashed ring, or drag the R tag to resize it, and watch Φ change only when one crosses.

Chapter 4: Electric potential

VB − VA = W/q0 = −∫E·dr. For a point charge V = kq/r; in a uniform field ΔV = Ed. Drag the A and B flags along the ruler and watch the work change.

drag flags A and B

W > 0: an outside agent must push. W < 0: the field does the work and the charge moves on its own.

Chapter 5: Capacitors

C = q/V, parallel plates C = ε0A/d, energy U = ½CV². A dielectric multiplies C by k. Drag the top plate to change d and the side tag to change A.

Chapter 6: Current electricity

Ohm's law V = IR, power P = IV, Kirchhoff's rules ΣI = 0 and Σε = ΣIR, and RC circuits q = Cε(1 − e−t/RC). Press play and watch the capacitor fill up.

Resistors: series vs parallel

Kirchhoff: Example 6.11 (Fig. 6.12)

Junction: I₃ = I₁ + I₂. Loop I: ε₂ = R₁I₁ + R₃I₃. Loop II: ε₁ + ε₂ = R₂I₂ + R₃I₃. A negative current flows opposite to the assumed direction.

Chapter 7: Alternating current

Series R-L-C: XL = ωL, XC = 1/ωC, Z = √(R² + (XL − XC)²), tan φ = (XL − XC)/R, I = V/Z. Maximum current at f0 = 1/(2π√LC).
